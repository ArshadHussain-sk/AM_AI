About AM AI Audio Lab

Developer & Creator

AM AI Audio Lab is created and maintained by **Arshad Hussain**. You can study and follow his projects directly on his GitHub Profile (@ArshadHussain-sk).

Core Separator Neural Engine

This web application utilizes **Meta's Demucs v4 (Hybrid Transformer Demucs)**. Demucs separates music tracks by employing convolutional encoders/decoders coupled with a transformer layer to process waveform and spectrogram representation models concurrently, achieving state-of-the-art separation fidelity.

Hardware Configurations

  • AI Model: Meta Demucs (htdemucs) pretrained models.
  • Device Auto-Detect: Detects if PyTorch can interface with an NVIDIA GPU (via CUDA) or Apple Silicon (via MPS).
  • CUDA Acceleration: Offloads matrix arithmetic to your GPU cores, executing audio separation up to 10x faster than CPU modes.
  • FFmpeg Support: Essential for processing compressed audio containers (.mp3, .flac, .ogg, .m4a) to output clean files.

Development Tech Stack

FastAPI backend with a sequential Python multi-threaded queue that handles jobs safely, coupled with custom Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend logic and Wavesurfer.js for dynamic canvas-based waveform previews.

Masterclass Audio Demixing

AM AI separates songs into pristine studio stems (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano, and instrumental) using Meta's Demucs v4 Hybrid Transformer neural architecture.

💡 Drag and drop any MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, or OGG file up to 500MB directly onto the workspace.

Choose Your Model Quality

Select the optimal neural engine based on your listening environment:

  • HTDemucs: Fast, balanced everyday separation.
  • 🎧 HTDemucs FT (Fine-Tuned): Recommended for headphones with maximum transient clarity.
  • 🎧 MDX-Net Extra: Extreme vocal isolation with zero bleeding.
  • HTDemucs 6S: Isolates Guitar and Piano independently!

Live Synchronized Studio Mixer

Once separated, step into the full live mixing console:

  • Master Play / Spacebar: Plays all stems in phase lock.
  • Solo [S] Button: Isolate only vocals, only drums, or only bass with one click.
  • Mute [M] Button: Create instant karaoke backing tracks or drumless practice tracks!
  • A/B Comparison Switch: Seamlessly switch between the original song and your stem mix in real time.
